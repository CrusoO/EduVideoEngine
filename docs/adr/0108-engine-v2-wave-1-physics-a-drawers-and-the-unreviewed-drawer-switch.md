# ADR 0108: Engine V2 wave 1 (Physics A: mechanics, waves, heat) and the unreviewed-drawer switch

Status: Proposed, 2026-11-11. The owner accepts it. Builds on ADR 0107 (scope, wave plan, licence policy, privacy rule) and amends its item 7 (the subject entry gate) with a switch. Touches no frozen file and no item of section 15 of the architecture. `docs/ENGINE_INTERFACE.md` is unchanged.

## Context
ADR 0107 stages Engine V2 in waves. Wave 1 is Physics A: mechanics, waves and heat for Classes 8 to 12 (`OBJECT-COVERAGE-8-12.md`, section 5.2, W1). The owner decided on 2026-11-11 that the build goes ahead now and that the physics teacher's review is recorded later, so review must not block the build. ADR 0107 item 7 says a subject's drawers cannot register at Pilot until the subject has a named reviewer. The two have to fit: the drawers are built and tested now, and nothing unreviewed may reach a learner by accident.

## Decision
1. **Which drawers.** The plan's W1 list has 20 types (p_vector, p_fbd, p_incline, p_motion_graph, p_projectile, p_circular, p_pendulum, p_collision, p_energy_graph, p_rotation, p_gravity, p_lever, p_pressure, p_stress_strain, p_thermo_pv, p_heat_conduct, p_heating_curve, p_wave, p_sound, p_dimension). All 20 are built, plus p_spring_mass (the plan grades it drawn today; it was asked for and is cheap). The plan's other drawn-today W1 type, p_particle_states, is left to the existing `particles` shape. 21 drawers in `packages/pipeline/objects/physics/`.
2. **Own code only.** Every drawer is pure TypeScript with no tool and no new dependency, so the source register is unchanged: `licence owner-original`, source `own-drawings`, no `toolVersions`. SymPy, NumPy, matplotlib and Schemdraw stay `unverified` and are not used. Where the plan says "RK4 sim" the engine's own fixed-step RK4 is used (pendulum); everything else has a closed form.
3. **One request kind.** A new object kind `physics` carries `figure` (the closed list above), `params` (numbers in SI units, booleans and enum values only, checked per figure by a strict schema in `physics/params.ts`) and `lang` (`en`). The free-body diagram keeps the wave-0 `free_body` request (forces as magnitudes and angles). Impossible inputs are refused with the rule they break (a launch that never flies, a vernier reading that is not a multiple of 0.1 mm, a class 2 lever whose effort arm is shorter than its load arm). The figure needs an area of at least 4 columns by 3 rows.
4. **Truth from computed numbers.** Every number on a picture is computed by a tested pure function (`physics/compute.ts`) and printed once, as a line `key = value unit` to three significant figures (counts without a unit are whole numbers; a window of a graph is a nice number printed exactly). The net force of a free-body diagram is the sum of its arrows, never a typed number. Arrow lengths are drawn to a drawn scale bar, so a length can be read back as a force.
5. **Read-back (Q4).** Each drawer's `readBack` reads only the drawn shapes: axis ranges from the printed text, curve points from the path, arrows from their ends and the scale bar, ticks for waves and instruments. It checks the printed numbers against the inputs (within half a unit of the last figure), the geometry against the closed form (every point within a pixel), and the physics of the picture itself (energy never grows in a pendulum, a total bar is the sum of the bars, the moments of a lever balance, the vernier division that meets the main scale gives the reading). A corruption set that every drawer must reject is in `tests/physics-corrupt.ts`: swapped axes, an off-by-one sample, a gain error, a reversed arrow (a wrong net force sign), a wrong unit, a typed number, and a corrupted integrator (explicit Euler drifts past 1 percent where RK4 stays far under it).
6. **The switch.** Every wave-1 drawer is registered `reviewed: false` (a new optional field of a drawer; absent means reviewed, as for wave 0). The router asks `ENGINE_OBJECTS_ALLOW_UNREVIEWED` (`flags.ts`, passed in by the seam, never read from `process.env`): an explicit value wins (`1`, `true`, `on`, `yes` on; anything else, a typo included, off); with no value it is on when `NODE_ENV` is `development` or `test` and off everywhere else. With the switch off, a request that an unreviewed drawer would draw becomes outcome 8 of the decision tree as a label card plus a line in the review queue, and a cached drawing is not served. With it on, the drawing is made and the provenance record says `reviewed: false` and the expansion notes say "drawn by the unreviewed drawer". When the owner records a review, the drawer is registered without `reviewed: false` (and a reviewer in provenance); nothing else changes. The switch gates nothing when `ENGINE_OBJECTS_V2` is off. The coverage report counts registered and reviewed pairs apart, so unreviewed coverage is never shown as done.
7. **Figures are whole.** The story language keeps 28 shapes of a scene, 16 points of a polygon and 600 characters of a path. A figure uses at most 24 shapes; a shaded area is a filled closed path; the expansion draws a figure whole or not at all and says why (so in practice one physics figure per scene).
8. **Accessibility (Q2).** Text is at least 2.4 stage units (the story default: 8.6 px on a 360 px wide phone), in the safe label alphabet of wave 0, inside the figure's area and clear of other text. Lines and text use colours with at least 3:1 (text 4.5:1) contrast on the page of both styles; a fill is light (opacity 0.5 or less) except for small dots. Two lines of one graph differ by a dash, a width or a label on the line, never by colour alone.
9. **Languages.** Labels pass through `LABEL_HOOKS` (`physics/kit.ts`), the identity for English. Hindi and Nepali labels will be tables passed through the spoken-maths normaliser of ADR 0106; no drawer builds a sentence by itself.
10. **Privacy scan fix.** The cache and queue scans read the 64-character hexadecimal hashes they hold as phone numbers about one time in five (ten digits in a row). `findPrivacyProblems` now sets a SHA-256 hash aside for the phone rule only; the secret rules still see it.

## What this wave does not do
No physics teacher has reviewed anything; reviewer names stay `[owner to name]`. No motion rules (`ENGINE_MOTION_V2` still gates nothing) and no mistake visuals. No convection or radiation icons, pulleys, rolling, error bars on a graph or the ear (the plan lists them under the same object names; each drawer covers the figures its read-outs and tests describe, and the gaps are listed in the pull request). No live model call, no network, no paid service. Chapter lists remain UNVERIFIED against the official syllabus.

## Consequences
- The physics objects of W1 can be drawn in development and in staging now; production keeps label cards until the owner flips the switch or records reviews.
- Reviewing a drawer is one line of registration, which makes the owner's review hours the only gate left (about 6 hours for W1 in the plan's estimate).
- A figure per scene is the working rule until the story language's 28-shape limit is raised (a change to a frozen-adjacent limit, not made here).
