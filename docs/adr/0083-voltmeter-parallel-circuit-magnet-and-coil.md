# ADR 0083: A voltmeter and a parallel circuit, a magnet and a coil, and forward laws in the compiler

Status: proposed (agent, at the owner's request of 2026-10-23: "add more physics drawings: a voltmeter, parallel circuits and a magnet with a coil, so electromagnetic induction can be built too"; the owner is asked to confirm this record). Supersedes nothing. The component list of the schema is closed, so `magnetCoil` is an ADR change; the circuit gains optional props, so no existing scene changes.

## Context
ADR 0082 gave the engine a circuit and a whiteboard theme. Electromagnetic induction (class 12; on the JEE and NEET syllabi) needs a picture of a magnet moving to and from a coil with a meter, and a lesson on it needs a law that is worked forward (an emf is worked out from the turns, the change in flux and the time), which the compiler of ADR 0081 could not do (it solved a given total for an unknown).

## Decision
1. **`circuit` gains `voltmeter` and `resistor2`** (both optional; `hidden` on the voltmeter shows `? V` as on the meter). A voltmeter is drawn across the resistor with a lead from each end to a dial of its own (in a parallel pair, from the two nodes through a dial between the branches). A `resistor2` turns the loop into two branches between the top and the bottom wire; half of the dots take each branch (the route of a dot is a function of its number, its place of the time alone). Labels are placed so none overlaps a part. A circuit without the new props draws exactly as before.
2. **`magnetCoil`** (the fourteenth name of the closed list; lazy chunk of 2.3 KB gzip): a bar magnet with its north pole (red) towards a coil of 2 to 6 turns joined to a meter. The magnet approaches, rests, retreats and rests, from the cue on the part `motion` (from time zero if none). The needle follows the rate of change of the magnet's place, not the place: it swings one way while the magnet approaches, the other way while it retreats and rests when the magnet is still. That is the point of the picture (the emf follows the change of flux). All of it is a function of the lesson time (ADR 0009); with reduced motion the needle rests.
3. **`magnet-coil-scene`** (the fourteenth library pattern; hook or concept; picture): the parts pop in, then the motion starts on the second sentence. It reads a passage with `magnetCoil` (new optional record field) and is usable only for a skill that has one.
4. **Forward laws.** A law whose unknown stands alone on the left of its relation (`E = N F / t`) is compiled by `compileForwardLaw` (`packages/content/src/kb/compile-forward.ts`): the worked solution is `E = 5 x 12 / 2`, `E = 30`, then `30 = 5 x 12 / 2` put back (the verifier proves the root and the substitution); the table shows the unknown for a third, two thirds and all of the quantity the law lets vary (`vary`); the mistake is dividing two of the givens and stopping; the concept claim is the knowledge base's own statement of the concept (a grounded, supported claim), so the lesson says what the knowledge base says. `Law` gains optional `vary` and `magnet`.
5. **Faraday's law is in the knowledge base** (`knowledge/chapters/physics-emi.json`, mock): `E = N F / t` with N = 5 turns, F = 12 webers (a change of flux), t = 2 seconds, E = 30 volts. The numbers are whole and small so the proportion table and the exact arithmetic work; they are not realistic magnitudes and the lesson does not claim they are.
6. **`pnpm lesson:make` passes the class** of the curriculum place to the build of a compiled skill (the planner request is keyed on the level).
7. **Budget.** The ceiling for a lesson with every component loaded rises from 46 to 49 KB (48.5 measured); the core stays at 35.5 KB (ceiling 36). The lesson of ADR 0081 loads 43.6 KB, the induction lesson 37.8 KB.

## Not built
- Lenz's law as a picture (the direction of the induced current, a second needle direction labelled), flux lines, a rod sliding on rails (motional emf), a rotating coil (a generator).
- A voltmeter for a series circuit with two resistors, switches, bulbs, a variable resistor.
- A parallel circuit lesson: the knowledge base has no law for it, and equivalent resistance needs rational arithmetic with several unknowns that the compiler does not template yet.
- Narration of the hook beat of the induction lesson beyond the engine's default (a hook may state no concept claim).
