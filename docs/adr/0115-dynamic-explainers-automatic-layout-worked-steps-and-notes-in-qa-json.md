# ADR 0115: Dynamic explainers: automatic layout, worked steps and notes in qa.json

Status: Proposed, 2026-11-22. Written during an unattended session; the owner accepts the ADR. Builds on ADR 0091, 0096 to 0099, 0102 and 0114. Touches no frozen file and no item of section 15 of the architecture. `docs/ENGINE_INTERFACE.md` changes additively (section 10); every addition is optional, and a request that does not use it is read and built as before.

## Context
A host (a model) can send the engine any `story`. Two real videos were made and read frame by frame ("why the sign flips", 48 s; findings in the PR description). The sanitiser already keeps what is valid and drops the rest, but five things stood in the way of "send any valid story, get a clean video":

1. Nothing measured text. Quality checks compared shape centres, so a line of text drawn on top of a row of circles shipped without a note, and a formula could run off the stage or sit in the caption band.
2. A worked-step scene (the commonest maths scene) needed hand-computed rows, sizes and cues, and a wrong step was marked by colour alone.
3. The soft notes (and the notes about what was dropped) lived only in `story/timeline.json`; the host reads `qa.json`.
4. Seven of the ten soft notes on a good scene were false alarms (a row of equal marbles, a bag drawn as a rectangle against circles, a one-letter label), so a host learns to ignore them.
5. `curriculum.grade` had to be 9 to 12 even for a story or a source, which never touch the knowledge base, so a Class 8 algebra story was refused. A role written as a colour (`color: "term"`) turned silently blue.

## Decision
All additive and on without a switch, because each one changes only what was wrong; a story with none of the problems builds to the same bytes (tests below).

1. **Automatic layout** (`job/story-layout.ts`, `resolveLayout`, run after the sentences are timed). It measures what is on screen at the same time (circles, rectangles, props, lines of text and formulas; the end of a move counts too) and moves a line of text or a formula, never a drawn shape, to the nearest free place inside a safe area (x 2 to 98, y 1.5 to 42, above the caption band). A line wider than the stage is made smaller (not below size 1.6). A label inside a box or a circle that stays put is left alone, and so is anything over a line or an arrow. When there is no free place it says `overlap_unresolved` and leaves the line where it is. Notes: `auto_moved`, `auto_resized`, `overlap_unresolved`, in `timeline.json` (`qa.layout`) and in `qa.json`.
2. **Worked steps.** A scene may carry `steps: [{ latex, say?, role?, wrong? }]` (at most 6). The engine stacks them down the middle with a size that fits their number, one sentence each, as ordinary `math` shapes before the usual sanitising. A `wrong: true` step takes the `look-again` role and is struck through with `\cancel`, so the mistake is not shown by colour alone. Dropped steps leave a note.
3. **Notes in qa.json.** What the sanitiser dropped (`story_note`), the layout notes, and the soft story and timing notes are written to `qa.json` as advisory entries (`ok: true`, `severity: "advisory"`, `kind`), the form ADR 0114 set. They never change the verdict.
4. **Less noise.** `shapes_too_close` fires only when round shapes touch or nearly touch, and never for a label on a line; `scale_imbalance` compares a shape only with its own kind; `anchor_missing` anchors by the first word of three letters or more; `shapes_unfilled` ignores a shape that has a role. `color` set to a role name is read as that role, with a note; a colour that is neither is noted and the default used.
5. **Class of a story.** `curriculum.grade` may be 5 to 12 for a `story` or a `source`; a `nodeId` or a `prompt` still needs 9 to 12 (the same message as before).

## Consequences
- Tests: `story-layout.test.ts` (no change for a clean story, a move that clears a row of circles, the later of two formulas gives way, never together means no move, a label in a box stays, edges and caption band, a moving shape's end, no free place, the measure of a formula, the QA noise), `story-steps.test.ts` (steps, a wrong step, limits and notes, role as colour, the class of a request). The guard hash of the pre-ADR-0114 story in `story-roles.test.ts` is updated once: the only note it ever held was the false `scale_imbalance` on a beam; the sanitised story, the placed story and every page hash are unchanged.
- The build key of a story job is not bumped: a finished job with the same `requestId` and key is still found as before. A retry that rebuilds will lay the story out with the new code.
- Risks: the width of text and formulas is an estimate (about one size unit per symbol, tuned on KaTeX output), so a very wide formula may be moved when it did not need to be, or a close pair not seen; a formula struck with `\cancel` is drawn by KaTeX and reads fine at 390 px but is not verified for other locales; layout never moves a drawn shape, so two big shapes on top of each other are still the host's to fix. Not built here (next): burning no network into the page (the animated page links Plus Jakarta Sans from Google Fonts even when Atkinson is chosen, which stalls a page load on a machine with no network), automatic caption chunking, a table or area-model shape, and a fix for scenes whose sentences start no shape (`dead_air`).
