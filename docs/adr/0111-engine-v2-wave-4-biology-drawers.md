# ADR 0111: Engine V2 wave 4 (Biology: cells, tissues, heredity, human systems, plants, ecology, evolution, enzymes)

Status: Proposed, 2026-11-12. The owner accepts it. Builds on ADR 0107 (scope, wave plan, licence policy, privacy rule and the biology art policy), ADR 0108 (the drawing kit, read-back, the unreviewed-drawer switch) and ADR 0110 (the whole-grid figure, the per-kind request). Touches no frozen file and no item of section 15 of the architecture. `docs/ENGINE_INTERFACE.md` is unchanged.

## Context
ADR 0107 puts Biology in wave 4 for Classes 8 to 12 (`OBJECT-COVERAGE-8-12.md`, section 5.2, W4). The plan expects biology to depend on licensed line art (Servier Medical Art, MIT and ISC icon sets). The owner approved the art policy for this wave on 2026-11-12: **only our own original geometric drawings** (schematics, flow diagrams, graphs, simple labelled diagrams). Servier CC BY 4.0 and the MIT and ISC icon sets are allowed in principle but need attribution plumbing and are **not fetched and not bundled in this wave**; no share-alike and no non-commercial source is used. So wave 4 takes no new dependency, no third-party artwork and no tool, and the source register is unchanged. The review by a biology teacher is recorded later and does not block the build (ADR 0108 item 6).

## Decision
1. **Which drawers.** 25 drawers in `packages/pipeline/objects/biology/`, one per figure of the request: cell (plant, animal, bacterium; `b_cell`), organelle (mitochondrion, chloroplast, nucleus, rough ER, Golgi; `b_organelle`), cell membrane and what crosses it (bilayer, diffusion, osmosis, active transport; `b_membrane`), tissue (squamous, cuboidal, columnar, skeletal muscle, smooth muscle, parenchyma, sclerenchyma, xylem; `b_tissue`), microbe (virus, bacteriophage, amoeba, paramecium; `b_microbe`), a stage of mitosis or meiosis with the chromosomes counted (`b_division`), DNA and RNA structure (`b_dna`), transcription and translation from a sequence (`b_dogma`), Punnett squares (monohybrid, dihybrid, incomplete dominance, ABO, sex; `b_punnett`), pedigrees of three kinds of gene (`b_pedigree`), photosynthesis and respiration pathways (`b_pathway`), ten pathway diagrams of human and plant systems (`b_system`: digestion, airway and gas exchange, reflex arc, male and female reproduction, immune response, vaccination, water transport, phloem, pollination and double fertilisation), the heart and double circulation (`b_heart`), the nephron (`b_nephron`), the endocrine glands (`b_endocrine`), the neuron (`b_neuron`), plant parts (leaf with open or closed stomata, dicot and monocot stem, root, flower, seed, germination; `b_plant`), food chains and webs (`b_web`), energy pyramids (`b_pyramid`), population and selection curves (`b_population`), the carbon, nitrogen and water cycles (`b_cycle`), trees of descent with a last common ancestor (`b_tree`), the classification ladder (`b_taxonomy`), enzyme graphs (`b_enzyme`) and PCR and gel electrophoresis (`b_biotech`). They cover 30 of the plan's biology objects by name, and one more (enzyme figures) that the inventory does not name.
2. **Own geometry only, own data only.** Every drawer is pure TypeScript, licence `owner-original`, source `own-drawings`, no tool, no `toolVersions`. Pictures are circles, ellipses, rectangles, polygons, lines and dots; no icon, no photograph, no traced or converted artwork. Data are facts of record stated in `compute.ts`, `flows.ts` and `parts.ts`, never copied text or figures.
3. **One request kind.** A new object kind `biology` carries `figure` (the closed list above), `params` and `lang` (`en`). `params` are numbers, booleans and closed lists, checked per figure by a strict schema in `biology/params.ts`; the exceptions are two or four letters of a gene (a genotype, checked against the letter of its character) and a string of A, C, G, T or U (a nucleic acid, 4 to 15 letters), which code checks letter by letter. There is no free text and no field a typed answer could ride in. The figure takes the whole stage grid, A1:F6; a smaller area is refused at parse time.
4. **Impossible inputs are refused with the rule they break.** A genotype with an allele of another gene, or the small letter first; a cross of one character twice; a child no parents could have (`AA` x `AA` with `aa`; an X-linked son who got his X from his father; a daughter without her father's X); a coding strand with U, with a length that is not a multiple of 3, or with a stop codon before its end; RNA with T and DNA with U; a pyramid in which a level holds more than the one below, or an efficiency of 100 per cent or more; urine that exceeds the filtrate; a PCR whose efficiency is over 1 (each cycle at most doubles the copies); two gel fragments of one length; a logistic curve that starts above its carrying capacity; a predator and prey started exactly at equilibrium; a claimed direction of diffusion or osmosis that goes against the gradient; active transport with equal amounts on both sides; mitosis in a stage of meiosis and the reverse; an odd diploid number; stomata on a part that is not a leaf; a pair of species that is one species or is not in the tree; two ionising groups of an enzyme less than one pH unit apart. The router turns any refusal into a note and no drawing.
5. **Truth from computed numbers.** Every number is computed by a tested pure function in `compute.ts` and printed once as `key = value unit` to three significant figures. The Punnett cells are the join of the gametes and the ratios are counted from them (3:1, 1:2:1, 9:3:3:1, ABO groups) and tested against enumeration; pedigree shading and carrier counts follow the genotype under each symbol, and the chance of an affected child is enumerated over every sex and allele combination. The genetic code is the full 64-codon table (61 sense, 3 stop, 20 amino acids, the known degeneracy tested). Chromosome and chromatid counts, DNA in units of C and which homologues go where are computed from a plan per stage and tested (DNA conserved, homologues kept in mitosis and meiosis I until anaphase I). Population curves are the closed forms of exponential and logistic growth, species-area and selection; the logistic and exponential forms are tested against fourth-order Runge-Kutta, and the predator-prey cycle is integrated by RK4 and its conserved quantity is tested. Enzyme curves are Michaelis-Menten (with a competitive inhibitor), a two-ionisation pH curve whose optimum is the mean of the two pKa values, and a temperature curve (Arrhenius rise times a denaturation factor) whose optimum is found by golden-section search and checked against a scan. PCR copies are (1 + e)^n. Gel distance falls by a fixed step for every tenfold change in length. Trophic levels of a web are 1 plus the mean level of the prey, the food chains are enumerated, the last common ancestor is the lowest clade holding both species, and the carbon in the boxes of each pathway is added up and must balance. The equation of each pathway is balanced from its letters.
6. **Read-back (Q4).** Each drawer's `readBack` reads only the drawn shapes. A labelled diagram is read back part by part (the numbers of every ellipse, rectangle, line and dot, not only its box), each label against its table, each leader line from its label to the part it names and to no smaller part, nesting (a nucleus inside a membrane) and stacking order (upper epidermis above palisade). A flow diagram is read back box by box (label, and the text inside its box), each arrow by the box it starts and ends in, its colour, its name and that no arrow runs through a box. On top of that: a Punnett cell must be the join of the gametes drawn at the head of its row and column; a pedigree symbol's shading must be what the genotype written under it gives; the drawn rods of a dividing cell are counted again, with lengths, so a separated homologous pair is caught; each base of a drawn helix must be paired with its complement with 2 or 3 bonds; each codon must read to its amino acid; the direction of an arrow of diffusion is worked out from the drawn particle counts; a pyramid bar's width is its log energy; the ladder of a gel is fitted by least squares from its drawn bands and printed sizes, and each sample band is read off the fit; the blood is red only from the lungs to the body, read from the drawn arrows; a food web is rebuilt from the drawn arrows and its chains are counted again; the ring round a common ancestor is checked against the ancestor found again from the drawn lines; the stem bundles of a dicot are checked to lie on one radius and a monocot's not. The corruption set is `tests/biology-corrupt.ts` plus the generic one of wave 1; every sample rejects every corruption that applies to it, and at least two apply (251 checks over the 125 samples).
7. **The switch is unchanged.** All wave-4 drawers are registered `reviewed: false`; the router asks `ENGINE_OBJECTS_ALLOW_UNREVIEWED` as ADR 0108 item 6 says. Coverage counts registered and reviewed pairs apart. The objects flags stay off by default and with `ENGINE_OBJECTS_V2` off the output is byte-identical (test Q1).
8. **Accessibility, languages.** As ADR 0108 items 8 and 9: text of at least 2.4 stage units in the safe label alphabet, strokes only in ink, soft, red and purple (3:1 contrast tested), fills light (opacity at most 0.5), two lines of a graph told apart by width or a label on the line, an affected pedigree symbol shaded and drawn with a heavier outline. Blood colour is never the only cue (the legend is printed and the boxes are named). Labels pass through `LABEL_HOOKS`. Read-outs are at most 22 characters, so they fit their column.
9. **Shared files.** New code is in `biology/`. Additive edits: `spec.ts` (the kind `biology` and its parse branch), `drawers.ts` (registers the 25), `index.ts` (re-export), `scripts/objects-sheet.ts` (`--biology`), `scripts/objects-coverage.ts` (a wave-4 line), and in the tests only `objects-spec.test.ts` (the example of each kind) and the two lines of `objects-physics-gates.test.ts` that counted every registered drawer as a physics one. A biology figure may use all 28 shapes a story scene holds (the 24 of waves 1 and 3 leave no room for the sixteen cells of a dihybrid square), so a figure takes a scene of its own; the biology wrapper in `bkit.ts` therefore does not use `kindDrawer`.

## What this wave does not do
- **Not drawn:** the eye and ear, the skeleton, joints and the sarcomere, plant groups and the alternation of generations, hydra, earthworm, frog and fish (the amoeba is drawn as a microbe), crops and apiculture, glucose, amino acids, peptides and nucleotides as structures, polymer chains, DNA replication, recombinant DNA and the fermentor (the PCR cycle and the gel are drawn), a dichotomous key (the ladder of ranks is drawn), the brain, blood vessels and their walls, heart valves, an alveolus in section, an antibody molecule, the menstrual cycle graph and the hormones of the cycle, the fruit and the agents of pollination, maps of hotspots, Earth systems (winds, cyclones), real micrographs and histology (marked `g` in the plan: they cannot be drawn truthfully), and realistic or 3-D anatomy. Hindi and Nepali labels are not there (English only, through the label hook). Several arrows of the carbon, nitrogen and water cycles are not named (the process names that fit beside an arrow are written; the number of named arrows is printed). Reproduction is a schematic of the path of the gametes, with neutral words; no body is drawn.
- **Limits of what is drawn:** Punnett squares do not model linkage, multiple genes beyond two, or sex-linked crosses beyond the sex ratio; a pedigree is one family of two parents and one to four children; a dividing cell has a diploid number of 4 or 6 and chromosomes are rods; a pyramid has three or four levels and its bars are on a log scale (and say so); the models are the textbook ones: a logistic curve has no lag, the predator-prey cycle does not damp, the temperature curve is an illustrative two-factor model and not fitted to any enzyme, PCR is ideal doubling at the stated efficiency, and the gel's calibration constants are chosen so the ladder fills the picture. A tissue is a schematic of a few cells; its labels are the parts a student must name, not every feature.
- **Not verified:** the data above were not read from a source in this session; they are standard statements from knowledge, checked only against the facts the tests encode (a table entry that disagrees with the real fact would pass if no test encodes that fact; for example the rank names of the four animals, the order of the glands from head to pelvis and the hormone named for each, the position of the endodermis and the number of xylem arms of a root are not tested against a source). The plan's chapter lists remain `UNVERIFIED` against the official syllabus. The frames were looked at in real Chromium for a sample of the 125 figures (the rest were drawn without error and checked by read-back, not by eye). No live model call, network or paid service was used.
- **Not reviewed:** no biology teacher has looked at any figure, and no one has made the sensitivity check of the reproduction figures that the plan asks for. The reviewer is `[owner to name]`. Every drawer is `reviewed: false`.
- Motion rules (`ENGINE_MOTION_V2`) and mistake visuals are untouched.

## Consequences
- The biology objects of W4 can be drawn in development and staging now; production keeps label cards until the owner flips the switch or records reviews.
- A scene holds one biology figure, on the whole grid.
- Coverage by name counts an object as covered once a drawer for part of it is registered; the list above says which parts are not drawn, and the gap list (`pnpm objects:coverage <inventory> --gaps`) names the objects no drawer covers.
- The art policy is unchanged: if the owner later approves Servier or an icon set, a drawer for the objects that need real line art (the eye and ear, the skeleton) needs attribution plumbing first.
