# ADR 0070: A graph that moves with a parameter, and that the student can drag

Status: proposed (agent, at the owner's request of 2026-10-22; the owner is asked to confirm). Builds on ADR 0068 (the `morph` action) and spec 2.2.0 (ADR 0067); changes no gate, prompt or frozen file. It does change the reference lesson's neighbours: the reference lesson is not swept (see Decision 5).

## Context
Every motion the engine had was one-shot and linear: fade in, draw on, pop, ring, dim. Nothing moved with a quantity, and the student could do nothing but press play and answer a question at a pause. 3Blue1Brown's explanations move a quantity (a ValueTracker in manim) and let the picture follow it, so the viewer sees a rule hold at every value, not at one. In the generated lesson the rule "the roots add up to minus b over a" was said over a static parabola that showed one pair of roots.

## Decision
1. **`functionGraph` takes `sweep`**: `{ from, to, min?, max?, readout?, roundTrip? }`. The graphed expression must be a quadratic in x (`quadraticOf` reads a, b, c with exact arithmetic and refuses anything else). The x-coefficient b is multiplied by a factor; a `morph` cue on the whole graph moves the factor from `from` to `to` (and back to `from` with `roundTrip`, so the graph ends as the equation on the screen says) over the cue's window. The curve, the root markers and the readout (b, and "roots add to ...") are set at every draw from the factor at that time: a pure function of the time, so seek, pause and replay agree (ADR 0009). With reduced motion the factor jumps at the start of the cue.
2. **Drag to explore.** While the lesson is paused, dragging across a swept graph sets the factor: the left edge is `min`, the right edge `max` (default: the two ends of the sweep). The component reads `sweepOverride` and `interactive` from its render context; the player holds the dragged values, shows a hint ("drag left and right to change b") only while paused, and drops them when the lesson plays or seeks, so the timeline is always what plays. Pointer events, not mouse events, so touch works.
3. **What is drawn is checked.** `verify` refuses a sweep on anything but a quadratic with a non-zero a, without exactly one whole-object `morph` cue, or (when roots are marked) with a root outside the window at the factors `from`, `to`, `min` and `max`. The bundle validator requires the pair (sweep and cue) and refuses both in a bundle of a spec before 2.2.0, as it now does for `carryFrom`, a morph cue and an equation's `from`.
4. **The numbers are honest, and are not a claim.** The factor moves through values the verifier never saw; the curve and roots between the ends are computed in floating point from exact coefficients. The readout shows b and the sum of the roots as drawn (a sum equal to minus b, at every moment: tested). The claim the lesson makes (sum of the roots equals minus b over a) is the stored, SymPy-proved claim; the sweep shows it, and proves nothing.
5. **The pattern.** `plot-and-mark-roots` sweeps its graph when the caller passes `motion: true` (ADR 0072 widened the flag from `sweep`) and the plotted function is a quadratic with two positive roots: b from 1 to 1.4 times itself and back, draggable between 0.6 and 1.4, on the sentence after the last root appears (held 3.5 s), with the window widened so both roots stay on it. The lesson assembler passes it unless the `motion` feature is off. Instantiating the pattern without it gives the scene that existed before, which keeps the reference lesson (spec 2.0.0, hashed, frozen) unchanged.

## Consequences
- The generated lesson's concept beat now has a sweep and a spec of 2.2.0. The planner and scriptwriter requests, and so the recorded fixtures, are unchanged: the pattern's slots did not change.
- Size. The graph chunk grew from 2.9 to 4.3 KB gzip and the core from 33.5 to 34.0 KB. The budgets in `packages/player/tests/budget.test.ts` are now 35 KB for the core and 42 KB for everything loaded (measured 41.0); the 60 KB hard ceiling is unchanged. Keeping `Sweep`, `morphProgress` and the quadratic helpers in files of their own stops the player core importing the graph chunk (ADR 0024).
- Only a quadratic with two positive roots sweeps today. A sweep of other families needs a new kind of parameter (a named term, or a function with a symbol), and the verifier work that goes with it.
- Drag is for a mouse or a finger on the graph; there is no keyboard way to change the factor yet (a gap for accessibility to close before a pilot).
