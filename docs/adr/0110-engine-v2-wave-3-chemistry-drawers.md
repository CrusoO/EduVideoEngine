# ADR 0110: Engine V2 wave 3 (Chemistry: atoms, bonding, organic structures, reactions, solutions, electrochemistry, apparatus)

Status: Proposed, 2026-11-11. The owner accepts it. Builds on ADR 0107 (scope, wave plan, licence policy, privacy rule) and ADR 0108 (the drawing kit, read-back, the unreviewed-drawer switch). Wave 2 (Physics B) is built in parallel under ADR 0109; this ADR touches none of its files except the additive lines named in item 9. Touches no frozen file and no item of section 15 of the architecture. `docs/ENGINE_INTERFACE.md` is unchanged.

## Context
ADR 0107 puts Chemistry in wave 3 for Classes 8 to 12 (`OBJECT-COVERAGE-8-12.md`, section 5.2, W3), and the owner says chemistry is the subject students find hardest. The plan names OPSIN, PubChem and RDKit for the structure drawers. The owner has not approved any of those tools or their licences, so wave 3 writes its own code, as wave 1 did, and takes no new dependency. The review by a chemistry teacher is recorded later and does not block the build (ADR 0108 item 6).

## Decision
1. **Which drawers.** 22 drawers in `packages/pipeline/objects/chemistry/`, one per figure of the request: Bohr shells (`c_atom_shells`), orbital boxes with Hund's rule (`c_orbital_boxes`), energy levels and spectral series of a one-electron ion (`c_bohr`), periodic table with the element cell (`c_periodic`), periodic trends (`c_trend`), s, p and d orbitals with their nodes (`c_orbital`), ionic bonding as electron transfer (`c_bond_ionic`), Lewis structure (`c_lewis`), VSEPR shape (`c_vsepr`), skeletal formula of a chain, ring or benzene ring with one group (`c_organic`), the isomers of an alkane (`c_isomers`), a balanced equation with its atom and charge count (`c_reaction`), mole and mass bars (`c_stoich`), reaction energy profile (`c_energy`), rate, equilibrium and Arrhenius graphs (`c_kinetics`), gas-law graphs and the Maxwell distribution (`c_gas`), titration curve with its indicator (`c_titration`), pH scale (`c_ph`), solubility curve and vapour pressure of water (`c_solubility`), a galvanic cell (`c_cell`), an electrolysis cell (`c_electrolysis`) and laboratory set-ups of distillation, filtration, gas collection and titration (`c_lab`). They cover 17 of the plan's objects by name.
2. **Own code only, own data only.** Every drawer is pure TypeScript, licence `owner-original`, source `own-drawings`, no tool, no `toolVersions`: the source register is unchanged and RDKit, OPSIN, PubChem, NumPy and the element-data packages stay `unverified` and unused. The molecule layout is this wave's own (a zig-zag with 120 degree bonds, groups in the free direction of their carbon, a circle in a benzene ring). Data are facts of record, stated in `compute.ts` and named in the table below, never copied text or figures.
3. **One request kind.** A new object kind `chemistry` carries `figure` (the closed list above), `params` and `lang` (`en`). `params` are numbers, booleans and closed lists (element symbols, metals, salts, families), checked per figure by a strict schema in `chemistry/params.ts`; the one exception is the formulas of an equation (`r1`, `p1`, ...), strings of letters, digits, brackets and a charge (`Cu^2+`) that code parses into atoms and refuses when they name no element. There is no free text and no field a typed answer could ride in. The figure takes the whole stage grid, A1:F6: a smaller area is refused at parse time (the physics minimum of 4 by 3 cells is not enough for these schematics and columns of read-outs).
4. **Impossible inputs are refused with the rule they break.** A molecule that leaves an unpaired electron (`CH3`, `NH2`, `NO`), exceeds the octet of a period-2 atom or needs more bonds than the atom has electrons; an ion that does not exist (`Na2+`, `Cl+`, any d-block ion); an equation whose given coefficients do not conserve an element or the charge, named; species that cannot be balanced or can be balanced more than one way; an energy barrier that is not above both the reactants and the products, and a catalysed barrier that is not between them; a zero-order graph that runs past the time the reactant is used up; a ketone with its carbonyl at the end of a chain, a double bond that is not between two carbons; a gas collected over water that dissolves, or by a method its density does not allow; a cell of two half-cells of one metal; a weak acid with no pKa. The router turns any refusal into a note and no drawing.
5. **Truth from computed numbers.** Every number is computed by a tested pure function in `compute.ts` and printed once as `key = value unit` to three significant figures; where three figures would mislead (109.5 degrees must not become 110) the value is written as a word. The balancer is an exact integer null-space over fractions. Titration pH is an exact charge balance at 25 degrees C, not the textbook approximations. The Maxwell distribution is normalised and its area is read back. Radial nodes are the zeros of the generalised Laguerre polynomial, and the count equals n - l - 1.
6. **Read-back (Q4).** Each drawer's `readBack` reads only the drawn shapes. Examples: the dots of each shell are counted and each must lie on its ring; the arrows in each orbital box must be Hund's filling; the printed configuration must add up to the electrons drawn on each shell; the highlighted cell of the table must be in the group and period that the atomic number gives; the drawn equation (its LaTeX is parsed again) is recounted for every atom and for charge; a graph curve is compared point by point with its closed form; a skeletal formula is rebuilt as a graph from its drawn lines and labels, every valence is checked and the formula is counted from the drawing; every isomer drawn is rebuilt as a tree and compared with the list of structures; a Lewis structure must hold exactly the valence electrons in its bonds and lone pairs; a VSEPR drawing must put each bond and lone pair in a direction of the arrangement its count of domains gives and the printed shape must be the one that count and the lone pairs give; the anode must be the metal of lower potential and electrons must flow from it; cooling water must enter at the lower end of the condenser; the delivery tube of a gas jar must end where the gas collects. The corruption set is `tests/chemistry-corrupt.ts` plus the generic one of wave 1; every sample must reject every corruption that applies to it, and at least two apply.
7. **The switch is unchanged.** All wave-3 drawers are registered `reviewed: false`; the router asks `ENGINE_OBJECTS_ALLOW_UNREVIEWED` as ADR 0108 item 6 says. Coverage counts registered and reviewed pairs apart, so unreviewed coverage is never shown as done. The objects flags stay off by default and with `ENGINE_OBJECTS_V2` off the output is byte-identical (test Q1).
8. **Accessibility, languages.** As ADR 0108 items 8 and 9: text of at least 2.4 stage units in the safe label alphabet (the equation is the one place LaTeX is used, `\mathrm{...}` so that a formula is upright, and its alphabet is checked), colours with 3:1 contrast, fills light, two lines of a graph differing by width or by a label on the line. An electron is a small solid dot inside a path and a graph marker a small solid circle (both have the contrast of a line). The page draws `dash` as solid (the runtime sets `pathLength` to 1), so a hashed bond is five short lines and not a dash pattern. Labels pass through `LABEL_HOOKS`.
9. **Shared files.** New code is in `chemistry/`. Additive edits to shared files: `spec.ts` (the kind `chemistry`, its `figure` and `params`, its parse branch), `drawers.ts` (registers the 22), `index.ts` (re-export), `scripts/objects-sheet.ts` (`--chemistry` for the samples file, and typesetting of equations with KaTeX as `story-video.ts` does), `scripts/objects-coverage.ts` and `coverage.ts` (the `--gaps` listing), `expand.ts` (the note says "a subject teacher's review" and not "a physics teacher's"), and in the tests only the lines that counted every registered drawer as a physics one (`objects-physics-gates.test.ts`) and the example of each kind (`objects-spec.test.ts`).

## Data of record used
| Data | Where | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Symbol, name, atomic number, standard atomic weight of the first 36 elements and seven more | `ELEMENTS` | standard values, rounded to the figures usually quoted |
| Electron configurations | `configOf` | the Madelung rule, with chromium and copper as the two exceptions of the first d row |
| Covalent radius, first ionisation energy, Pauling electronegativity for periods 2 and 3, group 1 and group 17 | `TREND_DATA` | standard values (radius in pm, energy in kJ/mol); a noble gas is left out of radius and electronegativity |
| Rydberg energy 13.6057 eV, hc 1239.84 eV nm, R 8.31446, F 96485, Avogadro, K_w 1e-14, molar volume 22.414 L | `compute.ts` | CODATA values |
| Standard electrode potentials of nine metals | `METALS` | standard values at 25 degrees C against the hydrogen electrode, to two figures |
| Indicator ranges of four indicators | `INDICATORS` | standard colour-change ranges |
| Solubility of three salts at 0, 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100 degrees C | `SOLUBILITY` | standard tabulated values to three figures; between the points a straight line |
| Vapour pressure of water, 1 to 100 degrees C | `vapourPressureKpa` | the Antoine equation with the constants 8.07131, 1730.63, 233.426 (mmHg, degrees C) |
| Molar mass, solubility class and the five gases of the collection figure | `GASES` | standard values |

Each value is computed, not typed, where a rule gives it. The tests check the facts the data must satisfy (for example the Balmer H-alpha line at 656 nm, 1.10 V for the Daniell cell, one atmosphere boils water at 100 degrees C, the Maxwell area is 1, the counts of isomers 2, 3 and 5 against an independent enumeration of trees).

## What this wave does not do
- **Not drawn:** crystal unit cells (the plan's SC, BCC, FCC and packing), polymer chains and repeat units, biomolecules (glucose, amino acids, peptides, nucleotides), coordination complexes and their isomerism, industrial process flow (Haber, contact, blast furnace), reaction-mechanism (curly) arrows, resonance, formal charges, ions in Lewis structures, hybridisation, orbital overlap pictures, a phase diagram, a heat map of a trend, real colours or photographs of reactions (the plan marks that last one `g`, cannot be drawn truthfully). Lewis structures are of one central atom with identical partners; skeletal formulas have one group on a chain of two to eight carbons; the table shows the cell and not the other symbols; the s, p and d figure draws one d orbital, `dxy`, with no phases. Covalent bond formation is shown by its end result (the Lewis structure) and not as two atoms coming together. Hindi and Nepali labels are not there (English only, through the label hook).
- **Not verified:** the data above were not read from a source in this session; they are standard values stated from knowledge and checked only against the facts the tests encode (a value that disagrees with the real one would pass if the test does not test that fact). The plan's chapter lists remain `UNVERIFIED` against the official syllabus. No live model call, network or paid service was used.
- **Not reviewed:** no chemistry teacher has looked at any figure. The reviewer is `[owner to name]`. Every drawer is `reviewed: false`.
- Motion rules (`ENGINE_MOTION_V2`) and mistake visuals are untouched.

## Consequences
- The chemistry objects of W3 can be drawn in development and staging now; production keeps label cards until the owner flips the switch or records reviews.
- A scene holds one chemistry figure (24 shapes at most, 28 in a scene), on the whole grid.
- A figure that needs more than the stage has (a mechanism, a crystal, a polymer) is a label card until a later wave builds it.
