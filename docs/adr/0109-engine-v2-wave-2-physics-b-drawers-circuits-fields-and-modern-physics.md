# ADR 0109: Engine V2 wave 2 (Physics B: optics, electricity, magnetism, modern physics, Earth-sky)

Status: Proposed, 2026-11-11. The owner accepts it. Builds on ADR 0107 (scope, licence policy, privacy rule) and ADR 0108 (the wave-1 kit, the `physics` request, the unreviewed-drawer switch). Touches no frozen file and no item of section 15 of the architecture. `docs/ENGINE_INTERFACE.md` is unchanged.

## Context
Wave 2 of the plan (`OBJECT-COVERAGE-8-12.md`, section 5.2) is Physics B: ray diagrams, circuits, field lines, induction, AC, electromagnetic waves, the photoelectric effect, atom models, nuclear physics, semiconductors and Earth-sky geometry (19 types, 40 chapter-object pairs of the inventory). The owner decided on 2026-11-11 that review is not a build blocker, that circuit symbols and netlist solving are our own TypeScript and that Schemdraw is not used.

## Decision
1. **Which drawers.** 19 drawers, one per plan type, all through the wave-1 `physics` request (`figure` plus SI `params`): `p_ray_mirror`, `p_ray_lens`, `p_refraction`, `p_prism`, `p_eye`, `p_interference`, `p_circuit`, `p_circuit_adv`, `p_electric_field`, `p_charge`, `p_magnet`, `p_em_induction`, `p_ac`, `p_em_wave`, `p_photoelectric`, `p_atom_models`, `p_nuclear`, `p_semiconductor`, `p_earth_sky`. Files: `optics.ts`, `eye.ts`, `circuits.ts`, `fields.ts`, `modern.ts`, `sky.ts`; numbers in `compute-b.ts` (re-exported by `compute.ts`); shared helpers in `kit-b.ts`. The wave-0 `circuit` and `ray_diagram` request kinds stay undrawn: a netlist or a device name without angles and positions cannot fix a picture, and a closed list of topologies can. (The router test of wave 0 keeps its stub drawer for `ray_diagram`.)
2. **Own code, no Schemdraw.** Symbols (resistor, cell, capacitor, inductor, lens, gate) are paths between two points; the solver is Gaussian elimination with partial pivoting over a modified-nodal-analysis netlist of ideal resistors and voltage sources (`solveNetlist`). Licence `owner-original`, source `own-drawings`, no tool, no new dependency; the source register is unchanged and Schemdraw stays `unverified` and unused.
3. **The circuit is read back from its own drawing.** `readDrawn` rebuilds the netlist from the drawn symbols and wires (which ends touch), takes each value from the number printed beside its symbol, solves it, and checks that every printed current and voltage drop is what the drawn circuit gives, that Kirchhoff's current law holds at every node, that Ohm's law holds on every resistor, and that each arrow points the way the current flows. A cut wire, a changed resistor or a reversed arrow fails.
4. **Ray diagrams are measured, not trusted.** One scale on both axes and a drawn scale bar. The read-back measures the object, the image and each ray off the drawing and checks that the drawn rays cross at the drawn image, that the image is where 1/u + 1/v = 1/f puts it, and (prism, refraction) that each drawn bend obeys Snell's law with the drawn face normals. The paraxial picture is used (the device is a line the rays bend at); the mirror is drawn as that line with a hatched back.
5. **Field lines by superposition.** Electric lines are traced from the field of the drawn charges, magnetic lines from a numerical Biot-Savart sum over the loops of a solenoid (a bar magnet is drawn as the same model and says so). Lines are simplified to at most 22 to 30 points and packed into paths of at most 560 characters. The read-back computes the field again at the middle of every drawn segment from the drawn charges (or the drawn coil) and requires the segment to run along it, the right way, to within 3 degrees plus half the bend; arrowheads must agree. Charge signs are drawn as paths (a label may not start with `+` or `-`).
6. **Integration and randomness.** The RC circuit is integrated by the engine's RK4 (`rcSamples`); the read-back compares the curve with the exponential to a pixel, so explicit Euler at the same step is rejected (tested). Nuclear decay is a seeded simulation (`mulberry32`, exponential lifetimes); the read-back reruns the same seed from the inputs and also checks the run against the decay law within 5 standard deviations, so a changed seed or a scaled curve is rejected.
7. **Refusals.** Impossible inputs are refused with the rule broken: an object at the focus of a concave mirror or a convex lens, equal indices, a prism ray that is totally reflected at the second face, violet bent less than red, a telescope whose objective is the shorter, slits closer than ten wavelengths, a circuit with a missing element or no unique solution, a window that does not hold the curve, charges less than 0.2 m apart, a potential of the wrong sign, a decay chain that runs out of protons, an electron that emits by going up. Total internal reflection, polar day and an unbalanced bridge are physics, not errors, and are drawn.
8. **Wave 1 limits addressed.** (a) Shared axis windows: `window` (interference, photoelectric), `window_taus` and `y_max` (RC) fix the window of a graph, so two figures in two stacked areas (`A1:F3` and `A4:F6`, each at least 4 columns by 3 rows) show the same axes; `compact` drops the RC read-outs that do not fit a third of the stage. The expansion still refuses a figure that would take a scene past 28 shapes; each wave-2 figure uses at most 24 and the RC pair 26. (b) The 16-point polygon limit is respected: filled regions are closed paths, the only polygons are the prism, the umbra and the 3-point arrowheads. (c) Three read-back parsers had to learn arcs and multi-part paths (`subpaths`, `numbersIn`).
9. **Switch, flags, provenance.** Every wave-2 drawer is registered `reviewed: false`; nothing changes in the router, the flags or provenance. `ENGINE_OBJECTS_V2` off gives the same story value (tested); `ENGINE_OBJECTS_ALLOW_UNREVIEWED` decides whether the drawings are made.
10. **Accessibility and language.** The wave-1 rules hold (text at least 2.4 stage units, the safe alphabet, 3:1 contrast for lines and 4.5:1 for text, light fills; small solid dots only in `ink`, `red` or `purple`). Dashes, widths and labels (`R`/`V` at the ends of the prism rays, `1`/`2` on two photoelectric lines) separate lines, never colour alone. Labels pass through `LABEL_HOOKS`.

## What this wave does not draw, verify or review
- Not drawn: the microscope (the eye drawer draws short and long sight and the astronomical telescope, so the object "Human eye, microscope, telescope ray paths" is covered in part), the reactor schematic (the nuclear drawer draws decay, chains and the binding curve), the transistor circuit (the semiconductor drawer draws bands, the diode curve, the p-n junction and six gates), the electroscope, the rainbow, the convection and radiation of wave 1, the magnetic field of the electromagnetic wave as a separate plane (E and B are drawn as two in-phase curves of one frame), resonance of a parallel circuit, meter bridge, mutual induction coils with real turn counts (the drawn loops show only which coil has more), and the sun path.
- The magnetic lines are cut by the frame of the picture, not closed; the line that hugs the winding is not traced because the field changes sign across it. The bar magnet is the solenoid model, not a measured magnet.
- Binding energy per nucleon is the semi-empirical mass formula without its pairing term: an approximation (poor below A = 20), not a table of measured values. Day length ignores refraction and the size of the Sun. The declination uses a sine, not the equation of time. The eclipse figure is not to scale along the Sun-Moon-Earth line and says so; it keeps the one proportion the physics is about (does the umbra reach the Earth) and the lunar figure is to scale.
- Not verified: no physics teacher has reviewed anything (reviewer names stay `[owner to name]`); the syllabus lists remain UNVERIFIED; nothing was measured against a textbook figure; speed gate Q10 not run. No live model call, no network, no paid service.

## Consequences
- The 19 types and 40 pairs of W2 are drawable in development and staging; production keeps label cards until the owner flips the switch or records reviews (about 6 hours of review in the plan's estimate).
- Registered coverage of the plan inventory goes from 43 to 83 of 409 pairs (20.3 percent strict, counted by object name and not adding the pairs the plan grades as drawn today); 0 are reviewed.
- Reviewing a drawer is still one line of registration.
